Who We Are

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today

ST VISHNA RRN SCHOOL is a CBSE-affiliated institution committed to delivering world-class education in a nurturing environment. Since 2005, we have shaped young minds with academic rigor, creative expression, and strong values.

Our state-of-the-art campus, experienced faculty, and holistic curriculum ensure every student reaches their full potential — academically, socially, and personally.

Our Vision

A center of excellence developing globally competent, socially responsible citizens.

Our Mission

Principal's Message

At ST VISHNA RRN SCHOOL, we believe that education is not merely the transfer of knowledge — it is the ignition of a spark that will light the way for a lifetime. Academic Programmes

From foundational learning to board-level preparation — a comprehensive, CBSE-aligned curriculum for every stage.

Pre-Primary (Nursery – KG)

Play-based, activity-rich learning that builds curiosity and confidence at the earliest stage.

  • Language & numeracy basics
  • Creative arts & music

Primary (Class I – V)

  • CBSE syllabus with NEP 2020 alignment
  • Project-based learning
  • Sports & co-curricular integration

Deepening subject knowledge with labs, workshops, and exploratory learning experiences.

  • Science & Maths labs
  • Computer Science
  • Environmental awareness

Secondary (Class IX – X)

  • All five core subjects

Commerce and humanities streams with practical exposure and entrepreneurial thinking.

Admissions 2025–26

Admissions are now open. Follow these simple steps to secure your child's future.

How to Apply

  1. Collect the Prospectus

    Visit the school office or download the prospectus. Contains all details about fees, streams, and facilities.

  2. Submit Application Form

    Fill out and submit the admission form along with all required documents to the school office.

  3. Entrance Assessment

    Students (Class III onwards) appear for a written test and interaction with the principal.

  4. Confirmation & Fee Payment

    Upon selection, receive the offer letter and complete fee payment to confirm the seat.

Eligibility Criteria

🧒
Age Requirement

Nursery: 3+ yrs · KG: 4+ yrs · Class I: 5+ yrs as on 1st April of the admission year.

📄
Documents Required

Birth certificate, Aadhaar card, previous school TC, mark sheets (Class II+), passport photos.

📅
Admission Schedule

Registration: Jan–Feb · Entrance Test: March · Results: March-end · Classes begin: April.

💰
Fee Structure

Competitive and affordable. Scholarships available for meritorious and economically weaker students.
